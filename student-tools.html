PLAN WHERE TO STAY

Hostel & accommodation finder

Choose a university to open its official website and check accommodation information.

Choose an institution to see its official website and a checklist for comparing accommodation.

Before paying for a hostel

  • Confirm the hostel address and owner or management.
  • Visit in person with a trusted adult where possible.
  • Ask for the total cost, utility charges, rules and refund policy in writing.
  • Request a receipt and avoid sending money to an unverified account.
  • Confirm whether the university recommends or manages the accommodation.
